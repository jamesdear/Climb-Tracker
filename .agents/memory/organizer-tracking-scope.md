---
name: Organizer tracking scope
description: Activity tracking is for organizers, not children using the competition.
---

Keep account-activity tracking limited to organizers; do not expand it to climber browsing without an explicit request.

**Why:** The user approved tracking organizer invitations and usage to see whether LCC staff have tried the app, with organizer-only scope.

**How to apply:** Distinguish invitation acceptance from link clicks and authenticated requests from active human attention. Do not infer historical acceptance times or visits from unrelated profile timestamps.
